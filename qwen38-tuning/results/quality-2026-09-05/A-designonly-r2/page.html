03 / PHILOSOPHY The mission, unchanged

We believe access to the world's information is a right — and that the best tool for it is a search box that simply works.

Google began as an academic project to rank the web by what actually matters: how many other pages point to a page, and how. That idea — PageRank — turned a messy pile of links into an answer.

Twenty-five years later the ambition is the same, only larger. The question is no longer "what's on the page?" but "what do you actually need?" — and the answer is increasingly generated, not just retrieved.

A short history of getting bigger.
