01 The mission The operating sentence

"Organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful."

It reads like a line from a mission statement, but it is closer to a spec. Every product below — the search box, the map, the inbox, the model — is one answer to the same question: how do you take something as large as the world's knowledge and make it reachable in a single keystroke?

04 A short history 1998 → today
  1. 1998

    BackRub becomes Google

    A Stanford backlink-analysis tool, named for "googol" — the 1 followed by one hundred zeros — outgrows its dorm-room origins.

  2. 1998

    Founded

    Larry Page and Sergey Brin incorporate Google, Inc., betting that relevance could be computed, not curated.

  3. 2004

    The IPO

    Google goes public. "Don't be evil" is written into the bylaws — and quietly removed a decade later.

  4. 2015

    Alphabet, Inc.

    A holding company is born. Google becomes the search-and-advertising arm of a much larger, stranger organism.

  5. 2023

    Gemini

    The first natively multimodal model arrives — the search box quietly becomes a conversation.
