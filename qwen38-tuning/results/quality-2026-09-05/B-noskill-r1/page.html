03 — Principles

How the work
gets done.

The 1998 ten principles, distilled to the three that still hold.

01

Start with the user

Everything else follows. If it doesn't make the user's day measurably better, it doesn't ship.

02

Fast is a feature

Latency is a product decision. Half a second of saved time, multiplied by a hundred billion queries, is a decade of human life.

03

Answerable questions

When in doubt, measure. Every claim on this page names the thing it came from — or says it's a hypothesis.
