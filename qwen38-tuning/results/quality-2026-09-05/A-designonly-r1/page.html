03 — How it works

Three moves, repeated a billion times a day.

Every product on this page is the same loop, tuned for a different job: find it, understand it, put it in your hand.

Step 01 — Retrieve

Find the answer in a fraction of a second.

Search ranks billions of pages in under a second, surfacing the result you actually meant — not the one that merely matches your words.

IndexingRankingSafeSearch
Step 02 — Understand

Read the world in text, image, and code.

Gemini and DeepMind turn raw information into reasoning — summarizing, translating, and generating across every modality at once.

GeminiDeepMindMultimodal
Step 03 — Deliver

Put it in your hand, wherever you are.

Cloud, Maps, Android, and Chrome carry the answer to the phone, the car, and the screen you're already looking at.

CloudAndroidChrome
