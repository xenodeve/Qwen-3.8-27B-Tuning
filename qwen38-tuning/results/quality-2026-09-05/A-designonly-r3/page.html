04 The arc

Twenty-eight years, five turns.

From a garage index to a holding company of moonshots.

1998

Backrub becomes Google

Two Stanford PhD students rename their search prototype after 10¹⁰ — and move it into a Menlo Park garage.

2004

The IPO

Google goes public, and “Don't be evil” is written into the company's founding bylaws.

2006

Two acquisitions

Android and YouTube both enter the family in the same year — one for the phone, one for the screen.

2015

Alphabet is formed

A parent company is built to hold the moonshots — Waymo, Verily, the rest — apart from the core.

2023

Gemini ships

AI becomes the connective tissue, threaded through Search, Workspace, and everything above.
