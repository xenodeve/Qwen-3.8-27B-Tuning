## What changed

d562806dbafae37109975e970aae91b43e73b440. Added an experimental comparison runner
that reuses the existing launcher/fixture primitives and records requests,
responses, hashes, actual prompt depth and server logs. NVFP4 production flags

with two skips. The bracket_matching quality fixture has a separate incorrect
escaped-quote expectation; its initial model failures are invalid scores and
the comparison screen now uses frozen toposort instead.

## What failed

The GSQ Thai-language gate fails on retained output, not on a dictionary counter.

Short request, GSQ without speculation:

> ทดสอบเปิดไฟล์ในชุดสำรองเพื่อ确认ว่าไม่มีข้อผิดพลาดในการอ่านข้อมูลหรือไฟล์ไม่เสียหาย

Deep original-session request, GSQ with MTP:

> ปัญหาภาษาไทยคือเมือโมเดลต้วอักษรไทยจะมไม้โท/ไม้ตรี/สระหายไประหวตและตวลตินปนเขามา ซึ่งเป้นขญาด้าน generation ท่ยแก้ด้วยการต้งค่าไมได้น

Another sentence in the same response:

> คณทดลอง PyThaiNLP แลวพบว่าไมwork ซึ่งสมเหตุสมผลเพราะ它是ตัวตรวจสะกดแบบดิกชันนารี

This establishes observable failures in this workload. It does not identify their
cause, quantify the failure rate, or prove that every GSQ output is corrupted.
The short "确认" leak reproduced verbatim in a second fresh boot with the same
seed, after routing GPU reads through the repository's UUID-based helper.

NVFP4's additional 200704-window replay also contains errors, including
"เมือรัน" and "แก้มคำเพี้ยน". EXL3's corresponding replay has awkward phrasing
but no observed burst of the same orthographic corruption. EXL3 retains its
production Han ban and Thai temperature cap; this is not an unguarded
artifact-only language comparison. Historical EXL3 corruption remains valid
evidence even when this one new replay does not reproduce it.
The normal 147456-window NVFP4 replay returned the same content as its 200704
probe; the observed errors therefore also occur at the normal allocation.

## What was NOT measured

No three-round paired speed verdict, GSQ draft-depth optimization, full agent
coding/reasoning/design evaluation, 100-turn soak, vision validation, or smaller
GSQ-variant evaluation has been completed. No quality claim is imported from the
publisher's benchmark table. The original incident depth was not reproduced
exactly. Shared-memory samples cannot alone distinguish pinned staging from WDDM
eviction, so full residency at the largest allocation is not certified.

## What remains uncertain

Whether a GSQ profile with language guards and tuned decoding is a useful
alternative remains open. Lower file size is established; an overall advantage
over either incumbent is not. End-to-end timings are exploratory, generated
lengths differ, and the initial incumbent runs overlapped download activity.
Do not convert those rows into a speedup claim.

## Decision

RESEARCH ONLY / NOT PROMOTION-READY under the current Thai gate. Stop expansion
to the expensive tuning, soak, vision and smaller-artifact tracks at this gate.
Finish the incumbent comparison rows and verification, retain the candidate and
all evidence, and leave existing defaults unchanged. This is a staged negative
screening result, not completion of every phase in the larger integration plan.
